# AgriSense

### AI-Powered Multi-Crop Market Intelligence and Decision Support Platform

## 1. Project Overview

AgriSense is a smart agricultural decision-support platform designed to help farmers in Tamil Nadu access crop market information and make informed selling decisions.

Farmers often face challenges in accessing market prices, understanding price variations, and identifying suitable selling opportunities. AgriSense aims to provide accessible market insights through a simple and user-friendly digital platform.

The initial prototype focuses on four major crops:

- Paddy
- Groundnut
- Coconut
- Banana

Users can select a crop and district to view relevant market information.

## 2. Problem Statement

Farmers may depend on intermediaries and limited sources of information to understand current crop market prices. This can make it difficult to compare prices and plan selling decisions effectively.

AgriSense aims to address this challenge by developing a centralized platform for crop market intelligence and future data-driven decision support.

## 3. Objectives

- Provide accessible crop market information.
- Support multi-crop price comparison.
- Enable district-based market information selection.
- Develop a simple and user-friendly interface.
- Explore machine learning-based agricultural insights.
- Support informed decision-making among farmers.

## 4. Target Users

- Agricultural market stakeholders
